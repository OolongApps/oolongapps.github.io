# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Sleep Timer: Turn Off Screen ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Android application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Data You Create

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

- Timer settings (duration, schedule preferences)
- App settings (preferences, configurations)

This data never leaves your device.

### b) Data Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates a third-party service that may collect certain data automatically:

| Service | Data Collected | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID, IP address, device information | Serving advertisements |

We do not control the data practices of this third party. Please refer to their privacy policy for more details.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We do not collect or process any personal information directly. The App:

- Uses timer settings solely to schedule screen turn-off at the requested time
- Stores app settings locally to preserve your preferences between sessions

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any of your personal data with third parties. The only data transmission from your device occurs through the integrated Google AdMob SDK as described in Section 1b.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All app settings are stored locally on your device using:

- **DataStore Preferences** — for app settings and timer preferences

This data is stored within the App's private storage sandbox and is not accessible to other apps. It is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App.

---

## 5. Advertising and Advertising ID

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can:

- Reset your Advertising ID in your device settings under **Google > Ads**
- Opt out of personalized ads in the same settings menu

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

- The right to access data held about you
- The right to deletion of your data
- The right to opt out of personalized advertising

Since we do not collect personal data directly, these rights apply to Google AdMob. For data collected by that service, please contact them directly.

To delete your local app data, you can clear the App's data in your device settings or uninstall the App entirely.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
