# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Dice Roller - Roll for boards ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Android application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Data You Create

When you record sessions, the following data is stored locally on your device only:

- Session start and end timestamps
- Dice roll results (individual rolls, statistics)
- App settings (preferences, configurations)

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it and share it yourself.

### b) Data Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates third-party services that may collect certain data automatically:

| Service | Data Collected | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID, IP address, device information | Serving advertisements |
| Google Play Services | App usage data | In-app review prompts |

We do not control the data practices of these third parties. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more details.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We do not collect or process any personal information directly. The App:

- Stores session data locally to show your roll history and statistics
- Tracks app open count locally to determine when to prompt for an in-app review

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any of your personal data with third parties. The only data transmission from your device occurs through the integrated third-party SDKs (AdMob, Google Play Services) as described in Section 1b.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All session recordings and app settings are stored locally on your device using:

- **Room Database** — for session and roll record storage
- **DataStore Preferences** — for app settings

This data is stored within the App's private storage sandbox and is not accessible to other apps. It is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App.

---

## 5. Advertising and Advertising ID

The free version of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can:

- Reset your Advertising ID in your device settings under **Google > Ads**
- Opt out of personalized ads in the same settings menu

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

- The right to access data held about you
- The right to deletion of your data
- The right to opt out of personalized advertising

Since we do not collect personal data directly, most of these rights apply to the third-party services integrated in the App. For data collected by those services, please contact them directly.

To delete your local session data, you can use the delete functionality within the App or uninstall the App entirely.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
